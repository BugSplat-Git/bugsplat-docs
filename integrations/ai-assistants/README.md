# AI Assistants

Connect an AI assistant to BugSplat, and it answers questions about your crashes from your BugSplat databases. The assistant reads crash reports, crash groups, versions, and symbol files, files a crash group as a defect in your issue tracker, and marks a crash group fixed. You sign in with your BugSplat account, and the assistant has the access you have in the BugSplat web app.
