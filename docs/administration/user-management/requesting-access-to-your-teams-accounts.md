# Requesting Access

If your team or company already has an account with BugSplat, you can request access from your team member using our [Request Access](https://app.bugsplat.com/v2/sign-up/team-access/) page.

Note that for security reasons, there will be no warning if the email you send to a team member isn't received. If you don't receive an email granting you access, double-check that you submitted the correct team member's email and try again. &#x20;

Your team member must take action to add you to BugSplat.
