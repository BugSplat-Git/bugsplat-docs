# Adding Users, Crashes, and Storage to Your Plan

Starter, Pro, and Studio include a set number of user logins, monthly crashes and errors, and storage. When your team needs more, you can add more to your plan without moving up to a higher plan.

## What you can add

Add-ons are priced at $10/mo per extra user login, $20/mo per 25,000 extra crashes, and $25/mo per 25 GB of extra storage, billed yearly.

| Plan    | Extra user logins | Extra crashes per month | Extra storage   |
| ------- | ----------------- | ----------------------- | --------------- |
| Starter | Up to 5           | Up to 25,000            | Up to 200 GB    |
| Pro     | Up to 15          | Up to 100,000           | Up to 250 GB    |
| Studio  | Up to 25          | Up to 100,000           | Up to 1.5 TB    |

Pro and Studio can also add console platforms (Xbox, PlayStation, and Nintendo Switch) at $299/mo each, up to three in total. Pro can add SSO for $75/mo and self-hosted symbol servers for $50/mo. Studio includes both.

If you need more than these limits, [Enterprise](../plans-and-billing-guide.md#enterprise) covers any volume. Email [sales@bugsplat.com](mailto:sales@bugsplat.com) to learn more.

## How to add more

1. Go to the [Billing](https://app.bugsplat.com/v2/company/billing) page in your account and click **Change Plan**.
2. Choose how many user logins, crashes, and how much storage to add.
3. Click **Update** to review the change and confirm it.

Only [non-restricted users](../../user-management/user-permissions.md) can change billing settings. If you can't see the Billing page, ask a non-restricted member of your team.
