# Updates to BugSplat Pricing Plans - October 2026

BugSplat now offers five plans: Free, Starter, Pro, Studio, and Enterprise. The full plan comparison lives in the [Plans and Billing Overview](../plans-and-billing-guide.md). This page covers what changed and what it means for your account.

## What's new

* **Five plans instead of four.** Starter, Pro, and Studio replace Team and Business. Studio is built for game studios shipping on console, and includes one console platform plus SSO and self-hosted symbol servers.
* **API and MCP access on every plan.** Free includes rate-limited access, and paid plans include full access.
* **Add-ons at checkout.** Starter, Pro, and Studio can add user logins, crashes, and storage without moving up a plan. Pro and Studio can add console platforms, and Pro can add SSO and self-hosted symbol servers.
* **Console support below Enterprise.** Xbox, PlayStation, and Nintendo Switch support is now available on Pro and Studio.
* **No overage billing.** When you reach your monthly crash limit, further reports are rejected for the rest of the month and you choose whether to add more. You're never billed for crashes you didn't plan for.
* **Team Spaces on Enterprise.** Enterprise organizations can run several teams under one account, with users, crashes, and storage pooled across teams and one org bill.
* **A new Free plan.** Free is now built for one developer, with 1 user login, 10,000 crashes and errors per month, 10 GB of storage, and 60 days of crash data retention.

## If you're on a Team or Business plan

Team and Business are now legacy plans. We aren't moving anyone off them: you can keep your Team or Business plan for as long as you like, and nothing about it changes.

When you're ready, you can switch to Starter, Pro, Studio, or Enterprise from the [Billing](https://app.bugsplat.com/v2/company/billing) page. Once you switch to one of the new plans, you can't switch back to Team or Business, so compare the limits before you change. A move to a plan that costs less than your current one can also include fewer user logins or less storage. For example, Starter includes 5 user logins and 50 GB of storage, while Team included 10 and 250 GB. You can add logins, crashes, and storage to the new plan to cover the difference.

See the [Legacy Plans Guide](legacy-plans-guide.md) for more on legacy plans.

## If you're on the Free plan

Teams that were on the Free plan before the new plans launched keep the previous Free plan's limits for 90 days. If your team uses more than the new Free plan includes, such as more than 1 user login, more than 10 GB of storage, or more than 10,000 crashes in a month, BugSplat shows a notice in the app with the date your team moves to the new Free plan and what it doesn't cover. If you need more than the new Free plan includes, Starter is the next step up.
